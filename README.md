## 🧑‍💻 About me

- 🎙️ I build **voice-first apps** that understand **English, Hindi and Hinglish** and turn speech into real actions.
- 🛒 Working on **[Countr](https://countr-one.vercel.app)**, an AI shop assistant that keeps the books for small businesses in India.
- 📡 I like systems work too: real-time **IoT telemetry** over MQTT with time-series storage.
- 🌱 Exploring open source through **Zulip** and **Oppia**.
- 💬 Ask me about **Node.js, React, voice UX, or Google Workspace APIs**.
